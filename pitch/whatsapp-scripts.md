# TrueCost — WhatsApp scripts for recruiting agents & landlords

Copy-paste as-is. Replace [Area] / [Name]. Keep messages short; one ask per message.

## 1. First outreach (agent — from Jiji/TikTok/Facebook listing)

> Hi [Name], I'm Obed from TrueCost — we publish verified rent prices for [Area].
> Every week, renters ask us to match them to rooms in [Area]. I'm giving 2 agents there their first 2 renter intros FREE.
> Want in? Just reply with the room types you handle. 👍

## 2. If they ask "what's the catch?"

> No catch. Listing is free, first 2 matches are free, and I never touch your commission.
> After the free ones, it's GH₵50–100 per serious renter — or GH₵300–500/month if you want a steady flow. You can stop anytime.

## 3. Landlord / caretaker (empty rooms = pain)

> Hi, I saw your [room type] post. I run TrueCost — renters searching [Area] ask us for verified options daily.
> I can list your place FREE and send you serious renters. First 2 intros free. Interested?

## 4. Follow-up (48h, no reply)

> Hi [Name], quick follow-up — I still have 1 free slot for [Area] this week. Want me to send the first renter your way? 🙂

## 5. After first successful match (convert to paid)

> Glad that one worked out! 🎉 Want me to keep them coming? It's GH₵[50–100] per renter, or GH₵[300–500]/month for a steady flow. Most agents pick monthly.

## 6. Featured-slot upsell (agent already paying per-match)

> Quick one: renters reading our [Area] guide ask for agents every week. For GH₵200/month I can put YOUR agency as the featured verified agent on that page. Want it before another agent takes it?

## 7. Renter intake (when a get-help lead arrives)

> Hi [Name]! This is TrueCost 🏠 You asked for help finding a [budget] place in [Area].
> Quick Qs so I match you right:
> 1. Single room or chamber & hall?
> 2. Self-contain a must, or shared OK?
> 3. When do you need to move?
> Reply and I'll send verified options within 24 hours. ✅

## Rules

- Reply to every lead within 1 hour when awake — speed is the whole business.
- Log every lead in /track (status: new → contacted → matched → closed).
- Never charge renters. Renters pay zero, forever — agents pay you.
- First 2 matches per agent are ALWAYS free. That's the foot in the door.
