The cited statutory text says that, for a monthly or shorter tenancy, more than one month&apos;s rent cannot be demanded in advance; for a tenancy exceeding six months, more than six months&apos; rent cannot be demanded in advance. The 1986 Act also sets the amended offence penalty. Read the sources above for the wording and context.

## What this article does not prove

The source record does not prove that a particular landlord is currently being prosecuted, that every residential property is covered, or that a complaint will have a particular result. It also does not document a market-wide figure for how many months landlords ask for.

## What tenants can document

- Get the advance demand and tenancy length in writing.
- Keep the agreement, receipts, MoMo records, and messages together.
- Use the official Rent Control channel or Ghana-qualified legal advice for a live dispute.
