Enforcement of the long-ignored 6-month advance cap is now active, and the rental market is adjusting in real time. Here's the practical version.

## What changed

Demands above 6 months' advance — the standard 1–2 years across Accra — are now being actively prosecuted. The law always said this; now the state is acting on it.

## What tenants should do

- Get every demand **in writing** (WhatsApp counts).
- Offer 6 months to start; only exceed it voluntarily with a written agreement.
- File at Rent Control if threatened — the portal is rentcontrol.mwh.gov.gh.

## What landlords should do

Formalise. Written agreements, receipts, and defensible pricing now protect you more than lump sums do.

> Bottom line: the market runs on paper now. Whoever keeps records wins disputes.
