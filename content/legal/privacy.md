## Privacy in plain words

We collect the minimum needed to run TrueCost: form submissions you send us (name, phone, area, budget, messages) and privacy-friendly analytics (pages viewed, no cross-site tracking).

## What we collect

- **Lead + contact forms:** the details you type, so we can respond.
- **Newsletter:** your email, so we can send price updates.
- **Analytics:** aggregated visits via Vercel Analytics. No advertising trackers.

## What we never do

We never sell your data, never share your number with agents without your consent, and never send marketing you didn't ask for.

## Your rights

Ask for a copy or deletion of your data anytime via the contact page. Newsletter: one-click unsubscribe in every issue.

## Questions?

Reach us via [/contact](/contact) — a human replies within 24 hours.
