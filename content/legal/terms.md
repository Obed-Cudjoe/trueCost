## The short version

TrueCost publishes editorial benchmark ranges, living notes, planning assumptions, and general information. A number may be date-stamped while still lacking a source log, sample size, or a confirmed asking-price versus paid-rent distinction. Treat it as a prompt for questions and negotiation, not a quote or guarantee.

## Benchmark and calculator limits

Area and guide pages show the provenance fields available in the repository. Missing fields are labelled and may require owner review. The calculator uses the displayed range midpoint plus clearly labelled planning assumptions; it does not establish the fee a landlord, agent, mover, or provider will charge.

## Property submissions and introductions

Partner submissions are reviewed before possible publication. Publication, availability, ownership, a match, a response time, or any rental outcome is not guaranteed. Confirm property details, fees, identity, and agreement terms directly before paying.

## No legal advice

Rights articles are general information. Their source/review status is shown on each page, and some claims remain marked for owner/legal review. For a live dispute, check the cited official source and consult the Rent Control Department or a Ghana-qualified lawyer.

## Content use and liability

You may share our guides with credit. Do not treat the site as a substitute for inspection, source checking, legal advice, or professional financial advice. We work to correct errors but cannot guarantee any figure, listing, source availability, or outcome.
