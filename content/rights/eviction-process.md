---
title: "No eviction without due process"
summary: "A landlord cannot lock you out, seize property, or cut utilities to force you out. Eviction needs notice plus a court order."
lawBox: "Legal eviction requires proper written notice, a filing at the Magistrate/Rent court, a court order, and enforcement by a court bailiff — in that order."
steps:
  - "If locked out or utilities are cut, document everything (photos, timestamps)."
  - "Report to the police and the Rent Control Department immediately."
  - "Do not vacate under pressure without a court order."
  - "Seek a lawyer for reinstatement orders where needed."
faqs:
  - { q: "Can my landlord change the locks?", a: "No. Self-help eviction is unlawful no matter what you owe." }
  - { q: "What if I genuinely owe rent?", a: "Owing rent starts a legal process — it doesn't cancel your right to due process." }
---

Threats are common; lawful shortcuts don't exist. Paper trails beat shouting matches every time.
