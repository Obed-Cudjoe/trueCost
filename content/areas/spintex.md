Spintex Road runs from Tetteh Quarshie toward Tema, lined with estates like Manet, Baatsona, and Sakumono. It suits workers who want space for less than Osu or East Legon prices.

> **Watch out:** road-facing "estate" listings are sometimes old compound houses. Always verify the exact junction before paying any viewing fee.
